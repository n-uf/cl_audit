My pick: V2 · Dot + stronger tint. The colour reads at a glance, as in the swatch grid you liked, and no key's shape or text changes; the name stays at 5.6:1 or more.

Notes

  • Tint, not fill. On Std the colour is deepened to 50% brightness before it is laid over the gunmetal key, so the white legend keeps its contrast; on Silver the colour is laid over the key as it is. Strength: V1 and V3 45% on Std, 22% on Silver; V2 80% on Std, 42% on Silver (the card header and Dock icon take a little less on Std: V2 55% and 60%).
  • By usage (Lab) keeps today's plain key, header and icon: only a graphite dot (#3f3f46, pale ring) so the name lines up with its neighbours; no tint, no foot line.
  • The selected tile stays latched down with its orange strip on top, over the tinted face; in V3 an orange profile has an orange strip above and an orange foot line below.
  • The picture in each tile is the Dock icon's. The tile pictures are drawn as today; if the Dock icon takes a tint (bottom rows), the tile picture would take it too.
