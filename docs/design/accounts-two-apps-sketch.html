Accounts: the Claude app and Terminal on different accounts

Main's Claude app is on wu@example.com; Terminal is signed in to zedekiah@example.com; ines@example.com is a standby account. Two names: the Claude app (its window, Code tab included) and Terminal (the claude command). Drawn with the site's CSS and the app's account boxes, at the card's two widths (519 and 420; stacked on a phone). This page holds only what was decided; today's panels are kept as the reference.

Decided
  • Settings sections: CLAUDE CODE goes. ACCOUNTS is its own section (one header, the engraved caps and hairline of every section; no separate "Accounts" label), and FOLDER holds Folder, Share from and Shared.
  • The split is a warning, in amber (Terminal's numbers still work): one amber zone from the panel's very top edge, inside its rounded corners, the ACCOUNTS header inside it, then one amber headline and two muted lines, said once, then the two accounts; the standby accounts below the amber. The boxes stay the normal dark; colour only in the lamps (both amber), the LCD badges (the signed-in half bright green, the missing half bright red: it points at the exact app) and the keys' small amber dots. Contrast on the amber zone: headline 7.3:1 or more, the muted lines 5.7:1 or more.
  • Headers: one line each. Every section header (ACCOUNTS, FOLDER, the rest) keeps its own hairline beside the title and has no separator under it; the first row starts 10 pt below.
  • In the split the ACCOUNTS header sits inside the amber zone with the same one line (tinted amber), and the headline starts 10 pt below it, as a first row would.
  • The zone's padding is the card's: 14 pt above the header and at the sides, so the header sits where it does in the normal state and the text lines up with the rows.
  • Red stays for "nothing works": signed out or expired (today's red IN USE box and its Sign in key).
  • Each row's key is the sign-in it's missing: Sign the app in on Terminal's account (today's Switch page: the window closes, its sessions move, you sign the Claude app in there); Sign Terminal in on the Claude app's. After either: one account, the usual green IN USE. Keys have no trailing "…"; menu items keep theirs.
  • Terminal's account isn't selectable; standby accounts keep today's selection (click, mint, SWITCH ACCOUNT).
  • Dashboard card and menu bar row: "Claude window on another account" in amber, a link to Settings ▸ Accounts; on the card Show window is amber.
  • One window key: the card's amber Show window and the Dock panel's are the same key (label, font, weight, spacing, dark text: 7.5–11.4:1 on the amber face), its window-open lamp a bright light with a dark ring. The panel's other keys are the card's keys too.
  • Dock menu: six items, no accounts, numbers or states. Dock panel: the card's language, compact keys only.

From the code
  • The Claude app's numbers can't be read. Usage comes from the usage service with a sign-in token (Sources/Reader.swift:309); the only one cl_audit uses is Terminal's, read-only from the Keychain (Reader.swift:228); the Claude app is read for ids only, never a token (Sources/AppSignIn.swift:25).
  • Sign the app in needs the Switch target (Switch.swift:48-68: the Claude app's account and organisation known, the account on the profile's standby list). The key is always offered; when something isn't known yet, the Switch page says what it still needs.
  • The move takes Code-tab sessions and scheduled tasks (SwitchPage.swift:365, 409-410), and the person signs the Claude app in; cl_audit never does (SwitchPage.swift:510).
