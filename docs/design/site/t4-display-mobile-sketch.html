cl_audit T4 · the Overview display on phones and tablets

Built on the branch. Narrow displays stack in three rows: OVERVIEW with the alert's sign right after it and the typed message at the right end, the title, the little Dock centred at the bottom. The first row has a fixed height, so the alert never changes the display's size. The alert is typed in for 3 s when a usage first turns red and at 100 %, then stays as a compact sign with the profile's name, right after OVERVIEW, while it is red. Wide displays keep one line. (The indicator idea that replaced OVERVIEW was dropped.)

Phone · scrolling: the display and the PAGE keys stay, the rest slides away

On a phone the menu bar, the window's title bar with the face keys, the brand, the display and the PAGE keys take about 300 of 812 px before any content. When the page scrolls down the menu bar, title bar and brand (and a page's own keys, such as the dashboard's Show keys) slide up out of view (about 0.25 s); the display and the PAGE keys stay, pinned at the top of the window: the title, the little Dock, a red sign and the page switch stay in reach. It stays compact while scrolling back up; only reaching the top slides them back. Tablets and desktops keep the whole strip. Built on the branch (phones, 600 px and narrower).
