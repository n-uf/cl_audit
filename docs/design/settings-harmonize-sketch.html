cl_audit · profile settings, one pattern

Settings ▸ a profile today uses several looks for the same kind of control, and puts a part's on/off away from its settings. Below: what's inconsistent now, the rules that replace it, and two layouts that follow them. Std face only; Black and Silver use the same parts. HTML only, nothing built. The switches, segments, tiles and pop-ups in the layouts can be clicked.

RULESOne pattern

  • Card: one title with ⓘ, then rows. Label on the left (same width in every card), value on the right.
  • On/off: the small switch, then a few words that follow the state.
  • Choices: segments for two or three short words; a pop-up for longer words or more choices; tiles under the label for pictures.
  • Explanation: ⓘ for what it does; at most one grey line under a row for what the current choice means.
  • Preview: one strip at the foot of the card: not running or no numbers yet · plenty left · half used · at limit · full.
  • A part that's off (Dock icon, Claude window, menu bar): A folds its card to the switch row; B fades the card's controls to 45%, as the cards that need the Dock fade today.

LAYOUT AEach part is one card, its switch first

Profile, Claude Code, Claude window and Alerts on the left; the Dock icon (behaviour, panel, look, preview) in the middle; the menu bar (look, preview) on the right. Switching a part off folds its card to the switch row, whose words say what's left. The Claude window card sits under Claude Code, because both are about how this profile reaches Claude and which account it uses.
