Dock icon menu: cl_quick's shape, one small menu

The Dock icon app's menu and the Dock tile's menu (and the menu bar icon's, under its row) follow cl_quick's menu: the profile with its light; its accounts as rows, the one in use ticked with its status line under it, each standby account a click away from the Switch page; "Sign in to another account…"; then the Claude window (a submenu), Quick chat, Refresh now, Dashboard and Profile settings. Rows are 40 characters at most. Shown here with Show emails on; sample profile Main, sample addresses ann@example.com, bob@example.com and cy@example.com.

The spec, row by row

  • Claude Main with the profile's light (its colour).
  • ✓ ann@example.com: the account in use, ticked; picking it does nothing.
  • Its status line under it: 5-hour 34% · Week 12%, 5-hour full · in 1h 42m, Usage hidden: Terminal on … (Usage hidden: Terminal's account differs when the address wouldn't fit in 40), No recent numbers, Signed out, The sign-in stopped working; a longer line of the record's is cut in the middle. Under it, each a grey line of its own when there is one, short and never cut: Credits · $4.20 of $50.00 spent while credits are being spent, and why the numbers aren't fresh where the status line doesn't say it: Agent not running · open cl_audit, Couldn't update · retrying, Usage service busy · retrying later. The full line keeps only the countdown (5-hour full · in 1h 42m), no clock time.
  • Each standby account, plain: a click opens the Switch page with it (the way back while a limit is full).
  • In a split, Terminal's account as its own row (bob · Terminal: a click switches Main to it, as the card's row does), and Sign Terminal in to ann@example.com… as the one extra action.
  • Sign in to another account…; then Claude window ▸, Quick chat, Refresh now; Dashboard, Profile settings….

cl_quick's menu, and where cl_audit differs

  • The same shape: the profile with its light, the accounts as rows, one grey status line under the account in use, a standby row a click away from switching, a row to add an account.
  • The account in use is ticked, with no "· in use" (cl_audit's rule; cl_quick writes "· in use" with a green dot).
  • The same addresses as cl_quick: each account as its whole address through the shared address rules (AddressPrivacy); never a name made from an address. While emails are hidden the menu bar's menu draws the blurred stand-in (AddressMenuRow, the address while the row is highlighted). The Dock draws the Dock menus itself and leaves out an item's view, so there a hidden address is the words hidden address 1, hidden address 2…: numbered so the rows stay apart, nothing of any address. Rows of at most 40 characters.
  • The menu bar's menu keeps its own row on top (the profile, its bars) and then these rows without the profile row and the status line, and Hide from the menu bar last.
  • Left out on purpose: the Switch account ▸ submenu and its "Experimental: may lose data" line (no submenu carries it now; the Switch page warns before anything happens), and the separate "Switch to …" while a limit is full (the standby row is that way back).
  • "Sign in to another account…" (cl_audit's words on the card's switch menu) for cl_quick's "Add an account…".
  • The Terminal row and its Sign Terminal in… (cl_quick has no Terminal split); the Claude window submenu, Refresh now, Dashboard and Profile settings… (cl_quick's app menu has Quick chat, Settings… and Quit instead).

Left out (they stay in the windows and panels)

  • The separate 5-hour and Weekly rows, "Updated … ago", "Sign-in renewed at…", "Sign-in: address", the long sentence.

the lighter rows tagged macOS are the ones macOS adds to every Dock menu; the rest are cl_audit's
