3 · What the switches do while things change

Opening and quitting take seconds. Pick a case and press play; time runs at double speed. Window cases are already built (commit fba1fc9): Before is the build before it, After is what you have now. Dock icon cases are proposed: Before is today's switch, which hides and shows and restarts the Dock; After starts and quits, and never touches the Dock.

Rules
  1. The switch moves at once and stays; its line says what is happening (“opening…”, “starting…”) and it ignores clicks until done.
  2. While something is changing the card looks every 0.5 s, so “open” shows within half a second of the window appearing.
  3. Each change has a time limit. Past it the switch goes back and says why, with one button (“Show”, “Retry”, “Pin”).
  4. No orange fix buttons while cl_audit is still in the middle of a change it started itself.
  5. A change caused by the Opening and closing answers is announced on the other switch (“quits in 8 s”) and ends neutral, not orange.
  6. NEWThe Dock icon switch starts and quits the app. It never touches pins, so the Dock never restarts; hiding lives in profile settings ▸ Dock icon.
