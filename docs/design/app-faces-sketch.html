Std
  • Every card is a plate with a printed label and an engraved rule; rows are split by engraved lines.
  • Text fields get an engraved line instead of a box; menus are dark recesses with arrows (the list that opens is still macOS's); steppers are − / + keys round a display.
  • Apply is the one primary key: aluminium with the lit orange slot, like Download on the site. Revert is a plain key.
  • Pickers (Panel design, Card style) are tracks with the chosen item raised. Opening and closing uses LED radios; "Together" results glow.
  • The Dock icon pictures themselves don't change: they are the icons' own designs.

Changes

Dock

Keep the Dock in orderExperimental

Pins the profile icons side by side, in profile order, with at most one Claude pin before them. Several Claude pins get mixed up between Claude windows; the profile icons always open the right one. Updates when you add or remove a profile.

⚠ Invasive and error-prone: it rewrites your Dock's pin list and restarts the Dock, and macOS can misplace or drop pins along the way. "Restore my Dock" puts back the pins saved before the first change.
