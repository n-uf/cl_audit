Switching accounts, smoother

Claude Main switching back from ultra-1 to ultra-2, with the seven fixes from your first switch. Same finish as cl_audit: alu keys, display windows, lamps, recessed readouts. Where it helps, it departs from today's layout. Std face; nothing built. The keys, the sessions question and the run states can be clicked.

  1. Only the account in use is lit: its lamp, its display and its coloured segments. Every other account is unlit: recessed, lamp off, its level in its own colours but dimmed and without glow. The one bright box on screen is the one in use.
  2. While the Switch page is open, a display appears flush on the right of the profile's tile: "Switch from / ultra-1", mint "Switching ● / to ultra-2" while it runs, "Switched to / ultra-2" at Done. Otherwise the tile stands alone.
  3. Switch from Settings or the dashboard card: select a standby account and its Switch account key appears. When a limit fills, the card has the one with the most room selected already.
  4. Sessions are asked every time, with no answer chosen for you. Moving them always brings a backup first. Each sign-in is checked before the switch goes on.
  5. Cancel stays in sight while a switch runs.
